# 004. Author Paywalls & Payments Architecture

Date: 2026-11-19
Status: Proposed (for interview)

## Context

ExoNovel authors may choose to publish works or individual story architecture parts freely—or place them behind a **paywall**. Because ExoNovel already involves `did:peer` identity and potential Web3 wallet integrations, payment architecture must serve both mainstream readers (credit/debit cards) and crypto-native users cleanly without forcing wallet friction on every reader.

## High-Level Story

1. **Author-Controlled Paywalls**: Authors decide per project, chapter/part, or story-architecture package whether content is free or paywalled (one-time unlock, serialized chapter pass, or subscription).
2. **AOTA Payment Rails (`All of the Above`)**: Under ExoNovel's AOTA principle, support **all** major payment options—**Stripe** (credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Connect payouts, Crypto Onramp), **MetaMask & 500+ Web3 wallets** via **Reown AppKit (WalletConnect)**, and **embedded consumer wallets** (**Privy** / **Dynamic**) for stablecoins (USDC on L2s)—keeping `did:peer` ownership decoupled from payment rail.
3. **Verified Entitlements**: Successful payment on any rail issues a unified entitlement record in Neon Postgres keyed to the reader's `did:peer` (and optionally verifiable as a receipt/capability token), granting access across Web, Desktop, and Mobile.
