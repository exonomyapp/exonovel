# 004. Author Paywalls & Payments Architecture

Date: 2026-11-19
Status: Accepted (AOTA multi-currency rollout approved; wallet & commercial controls placed in Profile)

## Context

ExoNovel authors may choose to publish works or individual story architecture parts freely—or place them behind a **paywall**. Because ExoNovel already involves `did:peer` identity and potential Web3 wallet integrations, payment architecture must serve both mainstream readers (local instant bank rails, mobile money, cards) and crypto-native users cleanly without forcing wallet friction on every reader. All **author wallet, payout, paywall pricing, and earnings receipt controls live inside the GitHub-style Profile page**, never Preferences.

## High-Level Story

1. **Author-Controlled Paywalls & ExoNovel Personal Sync Billing (Managed in Profile)**:
   - Authors decide per project, chapter/part, or story-architecture package whether content is free or paywalled (one-time unlock, serialized chapter pass, or subscription), and manage **all financial controls** across three dedicated Profile tabs: **Profile → Wallet** (payment rails & transaction receipts), **Profile → Paywalls** (author pricing & reader unlocks), and **Profile → Plans** (personal device sync subscription)—never Preferences.
   - **ExoNovel Personal Sync Billing (`$9/month` or `$90/year`, local currency equivalent)**: ExoNovel operates as a peer with its own **`did:peer` ID** and AOTA wallets to accept payments **only for syncing an individual user's own offline device content with their own web profile**. Writing on the web, writing on devices, and **collaboration sync between co-writers across web and device** remain **free**.
2. **AOTA Payment Rails (`All of the Above`, Non-USD-Centric — Tiered Rollout Approved)**:
   - **Tier 1**: **dLocal** (Pix, UPI, M-Pesa, SPEI, emerging markets) + **Stripe** (cards, Apple/Google Pay, BLIK/SEPA/Connect) + **Reown AppKit** (`USDC`/`USDT` across Base/Solana/Polygon + MetaMask & 500+ wallets).
   - **Tier 2**: **Razorpay** (India UPI mandates), **Flutterwave / Paystack** (Pan-Africa mobile money), **Alipay+ / WeChat Pay** (via Adyen/Airwallex), **TON Wallet**, and **Bitcoin Lightning** (sub-dollar micro-payments & non-custodial corridors).
   - **Tier 3**: **Airwallex** multi-currency accounts so author payouts settle in native currency without double FX conversion.
   - **Currency-Neutral Ledger**: Every price, balance, and split is stored with exact `(minor_units, currency_code)` rather than defaulting to USD/Euro, while keeping `did:peer` ownership decoupled from payment rail.
3. **Verified Entitlements**: Successful payment on any rail issues a unified entitlement record in Neon Postgres / Willow keyed to the reader's `did:peer` (and optionally verifiable as a receipt/capability token), granting access across Web, Desktop, and Mobile.
