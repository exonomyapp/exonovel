# 003-001. Top 5 Publication Formats & Natural Structures

Date: 2026-11-19
Status: Proposed (grounded in research; open for interview refinement)
Parent: [003-content](../003-content.md)

## Decision Summary

Support five foundational publication formats, each with a dedicated **Planning/Authoring Workspace** and **Published Output Structure** (detailed in [scope.md](../../scope.md)):

1. **Novels**: Story bible (characters, worldbuilding, outlines) + `Parts -> Chapters -> Scenes & Sequels` -> published front matter, sequenced chapters, back matter.
2. **Articles & Creative Nonfiction**: Angle/thesis, hook/lead, pivot, subheaded sections, research notes -> published headline, deck, byline, sections, citations.
3. **Short Stories & Flash Fiction**: Single-conflict anchor, *in media res* hook, iceberg backstory, escalation, flashpoint, resonance -> standalone piece or anthology entry.
4. **Screenplays & Scripts**: Logline, treatment, beat sheet/index cards -> sluglines, action lines, character cues, dialogue, parentheticals in 12pt Courier (1 page ≈ 1 min).
5. **Poetry**: Lines/line breaks, stanzas, meter/rhythm notes, collection sequencing (micro-chapbook, chapbook, full collection) -> line-accurate publication.
