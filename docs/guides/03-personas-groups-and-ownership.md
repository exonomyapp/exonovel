# Personas, Writing Groups & Owning Every Word

*Share your imagination with friends and readers without surrendering your signature.*

Half the joy of writing in school, clubs, or writer circles comes from trading pages—and half the headache comes from worrying who gets credit when ten people brainstorm in the same file. **Personas** and **Writing Groups** solve both problems together.

---

## Step 1: Create Pen Names With Personas

Maybe you write class journalism under your real name, paranormal mysteries under a pen name, and comedic scripts with friends under a group alias.

In **Profile → Personas**, you can create as many **Personas** as you want inside one account:

1. Give each Persona its own **display name**, **handle**, **short bio**, and **avatar** (uploaded cleanly inside the app—never opening awkward system popups).
2. Switch Personas anytime from your **avatar menu** at the top right of the screen.
3. If you haven't created extra Personas yet, that same dropdown reminds you with a single button straight to **Profile → Personas** so there is zero clutter until you need it.

Collaborators and readers only ever see the Persona you choose for that project. Behind the scenes, everything still ties safely back to you.

---

## Step 2: Start a Writing Group & Invite Friends

Open **Profile → Groups & Invites** when you want to team up on an anthology, school literary magazine, or shared universe:

- Create a group and copy a **personal invite link** in one click.
- Assign **Managers** for editors or club captains, and **Members** for contributing writers.
- Invite links automatically connect new members the moment they sign in—even if they didn't have an account yet.

---

## Step 3: Why Ownership Matters When You Collaborate

Inside ExoNovel, collaboration doesn't erase individuality:

- Every **scene**, **character sheet**, **timeline note**, **plot beat**, and **feedback comment** carries the creator's cryptographic signature.
- If a friend builds off one of your ideas or characters, credit stays visibly attached to you.
- When you decide a finished work is ready for readers, your **Paywalls** tab in Profile lets you choose what stays free for everyone and what unlocks for readers—with earnings flowing directly through the payment methods in your **Wallet**.
