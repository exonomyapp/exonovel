# Writing on the Web, on Your Device & Sharing Anywhere

*Write online, write offline in the library or on the bus, and collaborate smoothly either way.*

Writers don't work in just one place. Sometimes you're sitting at a school computer with a browser tab open; other times you're drafting offline on a phone or laptop without Wi-Fi. ExoNovel keeps both free—and makes clear what happens when two people collaborate versus when *you* sync your own devices.

---

## 1. Free on the Web

Sign in from any browser and write directly inside ExoNovel's cloud workspace. Your profile, preferences, personas, groups, and shared drafts stay ready whenever you open a browser.

## 2. Free on Your Device (Offline-First)

Install ExoNovel's device apps on **Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, or iOS** for free. Everything you write stays stored right on your own device so airplanes, dead Wi-Fi zones, and quiet cabins never interrupt a chapter.

## 3. Collaboration Between Writers Is Always Free (Web ↔ Device)

When you add co-writers or join a Writing Group, **collaboration sync between users happens automatically for free—even when one writer is working in the browser on the Web while another writer is working on their Device app**.

Exchanging original scenes, character blocks, and feedback between collaborators is part of how collaborative creation works in ExoNovel, not a paywall.

## 4. Syncing Your Own Device Content With Your Own Web Profile

Syncing **your own** offline device drafts continuously with **your own** online web workspace uses encrypted background synchronization, which is offered as an optional paid plan in **Profile → Plans** (**$9/month** or **$90/year**, or equivalent in your local currency, across cards, mobile money, and crypto rails in your **Wallet**).

In short:
- Collaborating with other people (even Web to Device) = **Free**
- Using ExoNovel on the Web = **Free**
- Using ExoNovel on your Device = **Free**
- Continuous encrypted sync of your own device library to your own web profile = **Optional Paid Plan in Profile → Plans**

---

## 5. Optional Exports to Google Drive & Docs

If a teacher, editor, or contest wants a file in Google Docs or Markdown, open **Preferences → Connected Apps & Google Docs**. Connecting Google Drive is strictly optional—you choose which folder to use, when to back up, and you can disconnect anytime in one click.
