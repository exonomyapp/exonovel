# Dear World: Welcome to Your New Writing Desk

*Pick up a pen—or open a blank page—without giving up what belongs to you.*

If you have ever stayed up late scribbling a scene in a notebook, trading Google Docs links with friends for a class project, or worrying where your stories really live once you hit "upload," pull up a chair. **ExoNovel** was built for you.

Most writing websites treat every piece of text you type as one giant blob owned by whichever platform hosts it. ExoNovel works differently. Here, every idea—a character you invent, a plot twist you sketch at midnight, a timeline, a stanza, or even a thoughtful note you leave on a classmate's draft—stays tied to you.

This short guide series (**Dear World**) works like a quick tour around a seasoned writer's table. We aren't here to lecture you on how to write. You already have stories inside you. We're here to show you how to use **ExoNovel** in a fresh way.

---

## Before You Begin

You don't need special software, servers, or technical background to start:

- **On the Web:** Sign in with your browser and land straight on your **Desk**.
- **Or on Your Device:** Use ExoNovel offline on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, or iOS whenever you want to write away from the internet.

---

## Five Everyday Ideas That Power Your Desk

When you look around ExoNovel for the first time, you'll see a few short words. None of them require an engineering manual:

1. **Your Desk:** The main table where you open projects, jot quick ideas in your scratchpad, and see what your writing groups are working on.
2. **Stories & Formats:** Whether you're drafting a **Novel**, an **Article or Class Essay**, a **Short Story**, a **Screenplay**, or **Poetry**, your Desk adjusts cleanly to the shape of your project.
3. **Story Building Blocks:** Instead of burying everything inside one endless document, ExoNovel lets you keep reusable cards for **Characters**, **Plots**, **Timelines**, and **Themes** right beside your chapters.
4. **Personas:** Pen names and genre hats—all inside one account. Write sci-fi with friends under one persona and poetry under another, without creating separate logins.
5. **That Short Key in the Corner (`did:peer:…`):** Look at the top-right corner of your Desk and you'll spot a compact badge you can click to copy. Think of it as your writer's wax seal—an independent signature that proves your characters, scenes, and edits came from you, even when you collaborate in a group.

## Try This in 60 Seconds

- [ ] Click **Desk** in the top bar and pick any format tile (**Short Stories**, **Novels**, **Poetry**, and more).
- [ ] Type a two-sentence premise into the **Quick Idea Scratchpad** and switch its writing language if you draft in more than one tongue.
- [ ] Click the compact key badge in the top-right corner of your Desk just once to see how copying your writer seal works.

When you're ready for the next step, move on to **Your Desk, Your First Story & Building Blocks**.
